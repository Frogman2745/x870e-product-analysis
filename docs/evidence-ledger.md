# Evidence ledger

The evidence ledger prevents sourced facts, modeled conclusions, and open hypotheses from being presented with the same level of confidence.

## Status definitions

| Status | Meaning | Appropriate use |
|---|---|---|
| Sourced input | Directly traceable to a dated product, specification, or price source | Describe the bounded comparison and its observed inputs |
| Modeled output | Calculated from documented inputs and persona weights | Inform a strategic hypothesis while clearly labeling it as model-derived |
| Validation gap | A decision-relevant claim that still needs market or hands-on evidence | Define the next research step and avoid overclaiming |

## Portable register

The complete eight-claim register is available in [`data/claim-register.csv`](../data/claim-register.csv). Each row records the claim, current evidence boundary, and the decision it can responsibly support.

## Expansion rule

The next release should add adjacent premium and upper-midrange products only after the highest-impact validation gaps have been pressure-tested. This keeps a larger comparison set from creating false confidence around unverified persona weights, temporary price promotions, or feature-quality assumptions.

The interactive presentation is available on the [Froghaus case-study page](https://froghausstudios.com/projects/x870e-competitive-analysis).
